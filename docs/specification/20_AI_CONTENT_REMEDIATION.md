# Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA — Optional AI Content Remediation + JSON-LD Guidance

**Status:** APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION
**Domínio:** `Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA`
**Dependency:** Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria + SCORE-GEO-002 + REPORT-SITE-GEO-001

## 1. Objective

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA adds an optional, downstream remediation layer that may propose exact website copy for evidence-backed content/semantic findings and may provide deterministic JSON-LD guidance per audited page/device.

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA is **not** a scoring stage. It must never alter retrospectively:

- `RuleExecution`;
- `Finding`;
- `Recommendation` generated by Priorização e recomendações;
- Score;
- Coverage;
- Confidence;
- Consolidation.

All Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA output is advisory and requires human review before publication.

## 2. Default and activation

Textual remediation by external AI is OFF by default.

Public controls:

```text
--ai-content-remediation
--no-ai-content-remediation
SEARCHGEO_AI_CONTENT_REMEDIATION
```

Precedence:

1. explicit CLI boolean flag;
2. environment variable;
3. `false`.

Accepted environment values are `true/false`, `1/0`, `yes/no`, `on/off` case-insensitively.

JSON-LD guidance is deterministic and remains available even when textual AI remediation is disabled or no AI provider is configured.

## 3. Editorial analysis context — YMYL / E-E-A-T

The AI layer may receive explicit audit context so semantic assessment and content remediation are not forced into one generic editorial profile.

Supported context fields:

```text
SEARCHGEO_CONTENT_RISK_PROFILE
SEARCHGEO_YMYL_CATEGORY
SEARCHGEO_PAGE_PURPOSE
SEARCHGEO_INTENDED_AUDIENCE
SEARCHGEO_EXPERIENCE_REQUIREMENT
SEARCHGEO_FRESHNESS_SENSITIVITY
SEARCHGEO_CONTENT_ORIGIN
```

All fields default to `auto`.

### 3.2 Context safety rules

The context is interpretive input, not a new score and not an official ranking-factor vector.

Mandatory behavior:

1. explicit values are audit context supplied by the operator;
2. `auto` permits only a provisional working hypothesis from supplied visible/persisted page evidence;
3. an AUTO inference must not become a persisted fact about credentials, professional review, personal experience, reputation, legal/regulatory compliance, editorial process or other hidden facts;
4. when an AUTO inference materially changes a conclusion, provider confidence should be lower than an equivalent explicit context;
5. when `risk_profile=ymyl`, the provider must apply a materially higher trust/evidence bar to claims that can affect health, safety, financial stability/security, civic/societal welfare or comparable significant well-being;
6. Trust is treated as the central E-E-A-T consideration; Experience, Expertise and Authoritativeness are relevant according to page purpose/topic and must not be required mechanically from every page;
7. `experience_requirement` distinguishes first-hand experience from subject-matter expertise rather than treating them as interchangeable;
8. `freshness_sensitivity=high` increases scrutiny of dates, periods and temporal qualifiers, but never permits manufacturing a newer date;
9. `content_origin` distinguishes content creator/source from hosting publisher when third-party, UGC or mixed content is involved;
10. language and market are context only and do not prove jurisdictional or regulatory compliance.

Consistency validation:

- `risk_profile=standard` cannot be combined with an explicit YMYL category other than `auto`/`none`;
- `risk_profile=ymyl` cannot be combined with `ymyl_category=none`.

The effective audit context must be persisted so a reopened report can prove which fields were explicit and which remained AUTO.

Primary conceptual references:

- Google Search Central — Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content: `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content`
- Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines: `https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/hsw-sqrg.pdf`
- Google — How AI Overviews in Search work: `https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/www.google.com/en//search/howsearchworks/google-about-AI-overviews.pdf`

These sources support the conceptual use of E-E-A-T/YMYL and page-purpose/user-needs analysis. RASAI must not describe E-E-A-T itself as a standalone official ranking factor or publish a fabricated E-E-A-T/YMYL probability.

## 4. Trigger contract

Textual Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA must be triggered only by eligible persisted content/semantic findings.

`Confidence LOW` by itself is **never** a trigger and must not be interpreted as poor content.

Eligible rule universe for exact-text proposals in this version:

```text
BR-GEO-028..033
BR-GEO-038..049
```

Structured-data rules are not sent to the free-form content remediation prompt. JSON-LD guidance is handled separately under the deterministic contract in this specification.

## 5. Evidence boundary

Each external Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA request is scoped to one persisted page snapshot/device and may contain only:

- normalized page URL;
- selected device;
- observed title;
- bounded persisted main-content artifact;
- effective editorial analysis context;
- eligible findings for that page/device;
- expected condition and observed value of those findings;
- persisted evidence linked to those findings.

Provider output must reference an existing supplied `finding_id` and evidence IDs that are a subset of that finding's persisted evidence IDs.

A suggestion with foreign or invented finding/evidence references is contract-invalid and must not be published.

## 6. Exact-text output

Each accepted suggestion contains:

- `finding_id`;
- objective of the change;
- target location/placement;
- exact proposed text;
- evidence IDs;
- suggestion confidence;
- mandatory human-review note;
- provider/model persisted separately.

The system must never write the suggestion back into the audited website.

## 7. People-first and anti-fabrication contract

Provider instructions must require content that improves real-user usefulness, clarity, completeness or trust.

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA must not ask a provider to:

- write only for search engines or generative systems;
- keyword-stuff;
- meet arbitrary word-count targets;
- apply artificial chunking solely for AI retrieval;
- fabricate freshness/date changes;
- invent prices, claims, guarantees, statistics, credentials, expertise, experience or sources;
- generate unsupported mass content.

As a local hardening rule, numeric/date/price-like tokens introduced in proposed copy must already be present in the supplied persisted content/evidence corpus. Otherwise the provider response is rejected as a contract error.

This numeric guard is additive risk containment; it does not prove that every nonnumeric statement is factual. Human review remains mandatory.

## 8. Provider routing

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA reuses the already configured Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria provider universe. It does not introduce separate credentials or model configuration.

Rules:

1. providers quarantined by Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria for the current audit are not reactivated for Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA;
2. explicit provider behavior remains single-provider;
3. AUTO may use the remaining healthy configured chain;
4. calls are sequential;
5. the first valid response stops the chain for that request;
6. provider success establishes URL provider pinning for subsequent Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA contexts of the same URL;
7. a failed provider is quarantined for the Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA routing session;
8. no automatic timeout retry;
9. selected `SEARCHGEO_DEVICE_CONTEXT` bounds the Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA request universe because only selected snapshots exist downstream.

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA provider failure is an auditor operational state and is never a website finding.

## 9. Telemetry

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA persists telemetry separately from Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria semantic-analysis attempts so cost by purpose remains auditable.

Minimum telemetry:

- URL;
- page/snapshot/device;
- provider/model;
- reasoning profile;
- attempt index;
- status;
- timestamps/duration;
- sanitized diagnostic class/status/code/request id;
- input/cached/output/reasoning/total tokens when reported;
- locally estimated cost/currency/pricing version when a supported pricing basis exists;
- request summary/hash;
- Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA contract version.

Raw API keys, Authorization headers and unbounded provider error bodies must never be persisted.

If a provider/model does not have a trustworthy local pricing basis, the report must display the monetary estimate as unavailable instead of fabricating a cost.

The context variables in section 3 do not create external calls by themselves and therefore do not independently create AI cost.

## 10. Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA run states

Persisted run states include:

```text
DISABLED
NOT_CONFIGURED
NO_ELIGIBLE_FINDINGS
SUCCESS
PARTIAL
NO_SAFE_SUGGESTIONS
DEGRADED
```

`NOT_CONFIGURED`, `PARTIAL` and `DEGRADED` do not change website scoring.

## 11. JSON-LD guidance when absent

For each actually audited snapshot/device with no persisted JSON-LD artifact, RASAI may materialize a conservative Schema.org `WebPage` baseline using only persisted/observed data.

Permitted generic fields include:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "WebPage",
  "url": "<canonical-or-normalized-url>",
  "inLanguage": "<audit-language>",
  "name": "<observed-title-if-present>",
  "description": "<observed-meta-description-if-present>"
}
```

An optional `mainEntity` may be included only when a single high-confidence persisted entity is sufficiently unambiguous and supported by the visible/persisted page evidence. The auditor must prefer omission over speculative typing.

The baseline must not invent FAQ items, reviews, ratings, prices, authors, dates, offers, addresses, credentials, product identifiers or any other property not supported by observed content.

A generic `WebPage` proposal is not a promise of Google rich-result eligibility.

## 12. JSON-LD guidance when present

When persisted JSON-LD exists, Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA does not replace it wholesale. It performs non-destructive generic review and may identify:

- JSON parse errors;
- identical duplicated blocks;
- absence of a document/graph `@context`;
- nodes missing `@type`;
- a `WebPage` node missing `url`, `name`, `description` or `inLanguage` when the corresponding value is already persisted;
- opportunity to represent page-level `WebPage` semantics without replacing a valid more-specific main type.

When no generic issue is detected, the report must say that type-specific required/recommended properties still need validation against the applicable feature documentation.

## 13. Structured Data external guidance

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA must communicate all of the following accurately:

- Google supports JSON-LD, Microdata and RDFa and generally recommends JSON-LD for ease of implementation;
- Structured Data must represent content visible/relevant on the page and must not be misleading;
- more-specific applicable Schema.org types are preferable when they faithfully describe the content;
- required/recommended properties vary by Search feature/type;
- valid markup does not guarantee that a rich result will be shown;
- Structured Data/JSON-LD is not a universal requirement for generative Search/GEO;
- RASAI must not invent special GEO/AEO markup.

Primary references:

- Google Search Central — General Structured Data Guidelines: `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies`
- Google Search Central — Intro to Structured Data: `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data`
- Schema.org documentation: `https://schema.org/docs/documents.html`
- Google Search Central — Optimizing for generative AI features: `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide`

## 14. Report contract

REPORT-SITE-GEO-001 is extended with:

```text
<AUD-ID>/report/content-suggestions.html
```

The page must use the same shared report navigation and external stylesheet as the other report pages and must remain visually consistent with the report design system.

It must show only analytically useful information, including:

- Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA enable/status/counts;
- effective editorial context and whether each field was explicitly configured or remained AUTO;
- explicit warning when context is partially/totally inferred;
- exact textual suggestions grouped by page/device/finding;
- objective, placement, evidence, provider/model and review requirement;
- compact AI telemetry for this stage: provider/model/reasoning, call count, accumulated duration, tokens and estimated cost when available;
- shortcut to the full AI telemetry page;
- JSON-LD proposal when absent;
- JSON-LD improvement notes when present;
- explicit advisory/non-scoring language;
- concise official-reference links relevant to the analysis.

Usability requirements:

1. use contextual shortcut links when a page contains multiple analytical sections;
2. ambiguous metrics or project-specific concepts must have concise tooltip/help text where it reduces interpretation risk;
3. tooltips/help text must supplement, not replace, an understandable visible label;
4. detailed telemetry belongs in `ai-usage.html`; the content page should expose only the summary needed for analysis plus a direct shortcut;
5. references should point to public official/primary sources whenever possible;
6. no page may imply guaranteed ranking, citation, rich result or regulatory compliance.

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA telemetry must also be visible in `report/ai-usage.html`, clearly separated from Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria semantic-analysis telemetry, with attempt-level provider/model/reasoning/status/tokens/cost/duration/error information.

## 15. Persistence invariants

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA tables are additive to `audit.db` and must preserve reopenability.

Minimum logical entities:

```text
ContentAnalysisContext
ContentRemediationRun
ContentRemediationSuggestion
ContentRemediationAttempt
JsonLdRemediationSuggestion
```

`ContentAnalysisContext` must preserve the effective audit-level values, which fields were explicitly configured, which remained AUTO, and whether the source mode was `MANUAL`, `MIXED` or `AUTO`.

Foreign keys must link suggestions to the already persisted audit/page/snapshot/finding universe as applicable.

## 16. Acceptance criteria

Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA is complete only when:

1. default audit makes no additional external content-remediation call;
2. default audit still produces deterministic JSON-LD review;
3. enabled Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA uses only eligible persisted findings/evidence;
4. invalid foreign evidence references are rejected;
5. unsupported numeric claims are rejected;
6. provider failures remain operational, not website findings;
7. Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA does not alter pre-existing score/findings;
8. `mobile`, `desktop`, `both` keep correct device scoping;
9. report site exposes the page with shared external CSS/navigation and consistent visual hierarchy;
10. editorial context is validated, persisted and exposed as configured vs AUTO;
11. invalid YMYL/context combinations fail before producing misleading output;
12. Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria/Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA provider prompts receive the context without changing the structured semantic output contract;
13. AI telemetry distinguishes Análise semântica por IA, roteamento e telemetria and Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA purposes and does not fabricate monetary cost when pricing basis is unavailable;
14. report exposes contextual shortcuts/tooltips/reference links without turning the page into a documentation dump;
15. unit/integration regression suite passes;
16. real Chromium smoke passes with Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA disabled and with Sugestões e remediação de conteúdo por IA enabled but no provider configured;
17. documentation/CLI/config glossary are aligned;
18. temporary validation workflow is removed before merge.
